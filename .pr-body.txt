## Summary

- The unbonded-offload probe was enabled on a WHOOP 5/MG and concluded the strap refuses the offload unbonded, permanently retiring itself. That conclusion was not supported by the evidence: all three probe attempts ended with `status=22` (GATT_CONN_TERMINATE_LOCAL_HOST) and `via=unknown` — the link was terminated LOCALLY by our own stack, not by the strap.
- The probe counted each local teardown as a strap verdict, charged it to the silence budget, and latched after three. A strap that holds links for hours without the probe was being retired on the basis of links OUR side tore down.
- The fix classifies the disconnect cause before charging the budget: a local teardown (status=22) is inconclusive and does NOT consume a silence-budget attempt. Only a strap-side drop (timeout, ATT error) charges the budget.

### Files
- `UnbondedOffloadProbe.kt` — `unbondedProbeLinkLostIsLocalTeardown(status, origin)` (pure, unit-tested) classifies the disconnect; `unbondedProbeLinkLostLocalTeardownLine` is the inconclusive line that does NOT charge the budget. Existing strap-side-drop lines updated to note they are now only for strap-side drops.
- `WhoopBleClient.kt` — the disconnect handler now checks `unbondedProbeLinkLostIsLocalTeardown` before choosing which line to print and whether to call `chargeUnbondedProbeSilence()`.
- `UnbondedOffloadProbeTest.kt` — 6 new tests covering the classification (local teardown with unknown/known origin, strap-side timeout, other statuses) and the inconclusive line (stage 1 and 2, origin naming).
